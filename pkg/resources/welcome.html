Epson TM-T20III Driver and Utility

This installs a native Apple Silicon and Intel driver for the Epson TM-T20III receipt printer:

  • Printer driver (CUPS filter + PPD) in /Library/Printers
  • TM-T20III Utility in Applications: printer settings, Printing Preferences and APD Utility
  • Command-line tools tmutil and tmqueue in /usr/local/bin

Connect the printer over USB before installing to have a print queue created automatically. The printer must be in USB Printer Class mode (the factory default).

Existing TM-T20III queues are upgraded and keep their settings.

Not made or supported by Epson.
