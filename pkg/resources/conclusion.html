Installed

If the printer was connected, a queue named TM_T20III is ready in System Settings > Printers & Scanners.

Printer not connected yet, or using Ethernet? Run in Terminal:

/Library/Printers/Epson/TMT20III/add-printer.sh
/Library/Printers/Epson/TMT20III/add-printer.sh TM_T20III socket://192.168.1.50:9100

Settings: open TM-T20III Utility from Applications.

To uninstall: sudo /Library/Printers/Epson/TMT20III/uninstall.sh
